5 — Storage bounds: the worked arithmetic

The two input_text helpers are bounded by construction against the verified 255-character entity-state limit (HA docs). Both helpers must be created with max: 255 — the HA default is max: 100, and both bounds (139 and 211 chars) exceed it. initial is left unset so the state restores across restarts (HA docs). Exact definitions are in design draft §4.3. The v3 draft stated the dispatch-log worst case as 237 characters — that was a miscount, corrected here. Both bounds below are reproducible from the format definitions.

Schedule snapshot — input_text.alerts_schedule_snapshot

Format: comma-separated MMDD<code> pairs, oldest first. Longest entry MMDDzmieszane = 4 + 9 = 13 chars. Cap: 10 entries.

1109zmieszane,1112zmieszane,1116zmieszane,1117zmieszane,1118zmieszane,
1123zmieszane,1125zmieszane,1130zmieszane,1201zmieszane,1202zmieszane
10 entries × 13 chars + 9 commas = 130 + 9 = 139 chars  ≤  255  ✓

Real example — the actual next 10 collections from the verified schedule (102 chars):
1109papier,1112bio,1116metale,1117zmieszane,1118bio,1123szklo,1125bio,1130metale,1201zmieszane,1202bio
The full 18-collection window is 189 chars — it fits today, but the design caps at 10 entries and does not rely on that.

Dispatch log — input_text.alerts_dispatch_log

Format: comma-separated key:status pairs, newest last; status one letter (P pending, S sent, F failed, U unknown, M missed). Longest key is a five-type garbage key: garbage- (8) + YYYYMMDD (8) + -bio (4) + -metale (7) + -papier (7) + -szklo (6) + -zmieszane (10) = 50 chars, plus :U (2) = 52 chars per entry. Cap: 4 entries.

garbage-20261109-bio-metale-papier-szklo-zmieszane:U,garbage-20261112-bio-metale-papier-szklo-zmieszane:U,
garbage-20261116-bio-metale-papier-szklo-zmieszane:U,garbage-20261117-bio-metale-papier-szklo-zmieszane:U
4 entries × 52 chars + 3 commas = 208 + 3 = 211 chars  ≤  255  ✓
Realistic mixed example (105 chars):
garbage-20261109-metale-zmieszane:U,garbage-20261112-bio:S,garbage-20261116-metale:S,
youtube-2026-11-07:S

Why 4, not 5: with 5 entries the five-code worst case is 5 × 52 + 4 = 264 chars — over the limit. The v4.1 claim that a 5-entry cap holds for any number of types per day was wrong: it only proved three types per day (5 × 42 + 4 = 214). The canonical code set has five types (papier, bio, metale, zmieszane, szklo); a five-code key is 52 chars. With a 4-entry cap the bound holds across the complete five-code vocabulary, with no assumption. A 4-entry log covers 2 days at the maximum two events/day, and up to 4 days at one event/day. The verified schedule has exactly one type per date; two types on a day is a safety margin, five is the absolute bound.

3 — Who gets what, and when

Anti-spam is part of the design: one message per event per day, no repeats, and an alert expires instead of lingering. If the schedule can’t be read, the tile says so out loud — it never just stays silent. The tile is the only monitoring signal for a stale schedule: there is no separate alert and no escalation in v1.

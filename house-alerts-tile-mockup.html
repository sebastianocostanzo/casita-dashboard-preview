2 — What the phone messages would say

The tile is for anyone glancing at the hall tablet. The phone message is what actually prompts action when nobody is looking at it. Wording below is the proposed draft, not final copy.

YouTube threshold

Counts only the time Home Assistant can confirm YouTube was in the foreground on the TV. The counter runs midnight-to-now in Europe/Warsaw and resets at midnight.

3 — Who gets what, and when

Anti-spam is part of the design: one message per event per day, no repeats, and an alert expires instead of lingering. If the schedule can’t be read, the tile says so out loud — it never just stays silent.

4 — What’s settled, what’s still a proposal

Not in this MVP
